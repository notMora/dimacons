## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-conversion, highly structured commercial hardware and construction materials platform. The brand personality balances heavy-duty industrial authority with modern transactional efficiency: direct, sturdy, organized, and unmistakably dependable. 

Targeting professional contractors, master builders (B2B), and domestic DIY renovators (B2C) in Cuenca and the southern region of Ecuador, the interface eliminates cognitive clutter. The aesthetic combines clean industrial utility with contemporary Swiss-style rigor: high contrast between deep carbon black surfaces and clinical whites, sharp technical data organization, compact micro-radii on interactive items, and high-visibility industrial amber accents to trigger immediate commercial purchasing actions. WhatsApp green is reserved exclusively for fast quotes and immediate contractor communication.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for maximum contrast, catalog legibility, and unmistakable commercial hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#F59E0B`)**: Industrial safety amber. Used strategically for primary commercial actions, badge highlights, cart additions, price callouts, and key interaction cues. It commands immediate focus without feeling playful or frivolous.
- **Secondary (`#111827`)**: Deep carbon slate. Provides the structural backbone of typography, active filters, technical tables, and high-contrast structural chrome (header, navigation banners, dark badge markers).
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`)**: Direct quotation green. Exclusively dedicated to direct instant communication, floating quoting buttons, and order routing via WhatsApp. It is never diluted for general UI state coloring.
- **Neutral & Surface Foundations**:
  - Main background: `#FFFFFF` (pure white for pristine product photography).
  - Subtle tier / Card canvas: `#F9FAFB` and `#F3F4F6` (engineered gray foundation).
  - Outlines and separators: `#E5E7EB` (crisp 1px boundary lines).
  - Neutral text & meta values: `#4B5563` and `#71717A` for skimmable specifications and SKU tags.

## Typography

The type scale utilizes Inter exclusively to maintain tight, utilitarian, and predictable metric rhythm across high-density product grids and commercial tables.

- **Headlines & Category Titles**: High font weight (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking to give product families and promotional headers a grounded, industrial presence.
- **Numbers, Prices & SKUs**: Rendered using tabular figures (`tnum`) to align pricing, bulk tier discounts, metric dimensions, and inventory counts in data-heavy procurement views.
- **Labels & Badges**: Upper-case or semibold compact micro-typography (`label-technical`) for standard packaging units (e.g., "SACO 50KG", "VARILLA 12MM", "EN STOCK").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid with precise technical boundaries to support dense commercial exploration:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, 64px (`4rem`) outer margins. Max canvas container constrained to 1440px to ensure catalog items do not stretch excessively on ultra-wide screens.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Reflows filtering tools into a persistent drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Product cards transition into a clean 2-column grid or single-row full-width transactional list.

Rhythm is based on a strict 4px/8px modular base scale, prioritizing compact vertical density for technical catalogs so users can view more specifications without excessive scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp hairline boundaries coupled with functional, tightly controlled ambient shadows. Rather than ethereal floating planes, elevation signals clickability, overlay dominance, and physical containment:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: `#FFFFFF` or `#F9FAFB` background bordered by a 1px solid stroke in `#E5E7EB`. Used for base product cards, filter bars, and specification tables.
- **Level 1 (Card Hover / Subtle Surface)**: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`. Applied on hovered product cards, small interactive chips, and dropdown listings.
- **Level 2 (Popovers & Filter Flyouts)**: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & WhatsApp Direct Float)**: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`. Anchors the persistent WhatsApp quote trigger and wholesale bulk request modals firmly above catalog layers.

## Shapes

The design system employs micro-radii (level 1: `0.25rem` / 4px default) to communicate industrial robustness, precision engineering, and structural stability. 

- **Containers & Cards**: Finished with an exact 4px radius (`rounded`) and 1px border. No exaggerated organic roundness.
- **Input Fields & Buttons**: Fixed at 4px radius.
- **Technical Badges & Spec Chips**: Fixed at 2px or 4px corner radius.
- **Exception**: The floating WhatsApp CTA button uses a full circular geometry (`rounded-full`) to differentiate it as an omni-present communication lifeline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial CTA**: Background `#F59E0B`, text `#111827` (bold weight), border `1px solid #D97706`. On hover: `#D97706` background. Used for cart additions, checkout, and major conversions.
- **WhatsApp Quote Action**: Background `#25D366`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none. Features the recognizable messaging glyph. Used exclusively for "Cotizar por WhatsApp" or bulk commercial assistance.
- **Secondary Technical**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#111827`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`. On hover: `#F3F4F6`.
- **Sizes**: Standardized to `36px` (compact/table), `42px` (standard/card), and `48px` (large/hero).

### Product Cards
- Contained within a 1px border `#E5E7EB` over `#FFFFFF`.
- Features product image on a neutral `#F9FAFB` square frame with SKU / Brand tags displayed in `label-technical` at the top left.
- Inventory status tag (e.g., "Disponible en bodega", "Bajo pedido") positioned near the price.
- Prominent price display (`headline-md`) with dual display when applicable: unit retail price alongside wholesale volume price.
- Quick action row with dual options: "Añadir al Carrito" (Amber) and direct single-click "Cotizar" (WhatsApp icon).

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Specs**: Light gray container (`#F3F4F6`), dark text (`#374151`), 4px radius, `label-md`. Used for dimensions, weight ratings, and materials (e.g., "Calibre 14", "PVC Sanitario").
- **Promotional / Clearance**: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FCD34D`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- 42px height, 4px corner radius.
- Border: `1px solid #D1D5DB`, background: `#FFFFFF`.
- Focused state: Border changes to `#111827` with a 2px outer outline in `#F59E0B` (amber safety ring).
- Search input includes clear category prefix dropdown and high-visibility search trigger button.

### Checkboxes & Filters
- Compact 18px square checkboxes with 2px corner radius. Checked background: `#111827` with white checkmark.
- Filter panels organized in collapsible industrial accordions separated by 1px `#E5E7EB` rules.

### WhatsApp Persistent Trigger
- Bottom-right fixed floating bubble (`#25D366`), white icon, paired with an optional dismissible prompt pill: *"¿Cotización inmediata para obra? Escríbenos"*.
